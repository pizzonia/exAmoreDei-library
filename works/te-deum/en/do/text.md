# Te Deum {#main}

[1]{.v} We praise thee, O God, \* we acknowledge thee to be the Lord.\
[2]{.v} All the earth doth worship thee, \* the Father everlasting.\
[3]{.v} To thee all Angels cry aloud, \* the Heavens, and all the Powers therein.\
[4]{.v} To thee Cherubim and Seraphim \* continually do cry.\
[5]{.v} [bow head]{.rubric} Holy, Holy, Holy \* Lord God of Sabaoth;\
[6]{.v} Heaven and earth are full \* of the Majesty of thy glory.\
[7]{.v} The glorious company of the Apostles \* praise thee.\
[8]{.v} The goodly fellowship of the Prophets \* praise thee.\
[9]{.v} The noble army of Martyrs \* praise thee.\
[10]{.v} The holy Church throughout all the world \* doth acknowledge thee;\
[11]{.v} The Father, \* of an infinite Majesty.\
[12]{.v} Thine honourable, true, \* and only Son;\
[13]{.v} Also the Holy Ghost, \* the Comforter.\
[14]{.v} Thou art the King of Glory, \* O Christ.\
[15]{.v} Thou art the everlasting \* Son of the Father.\
[16]{.v} [During the following verse all make a profound bow:]{.rubric} When thou tookest upon thee to deliver man, \* thou didst not abhor the Virgin's womb.\
[17]{.v} When thou hadst overcome the sharpness of death, \* thou didst open the Kingdom of Heaven to all believers.\
[18]{.v} Thou sittest at the right hand of God, \* in the glory of the Father.\
[19]{.v} We believe that thou shalt come \* to be our Judge.\
[20]{.v} [Kneel for the following verse]{.rubric} We therefore pray thee, help thy servants, \* whom thou hast redeemed with thy precious Blood.\
[21]{.v} Make them to be numbered with thy Saints, \* in glory everlasting.\
[22]{.v} O Lord, save thy people, \* and bless thine heritage.\
[23]{.v} Govern them, \* and lift them up for ever.\
[24]{.v} Day by day \* we magnify thee;\
[25]{.v} [During the following verse, by local custom, all make a profound bow.]{.rubric} And we worship thy Name \* ever, world without end.\
[26]{.v} Vouchsafe, O Lord, to keep us \* this day without sin.\
[27]{.v} O Lord, have mercy upon us, \* have mercy upon us.\
[28]{.v} O Lord, let thy mercy lighten upon us, \* as our trust is in thee.\
[29]{.v} O Lord, in thee have I trusted, \* let me never be confounded.
