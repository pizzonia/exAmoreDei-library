# Regina caeli {#main}

[1]{.v} O Queen of heaven rejoice! alleluia:\
For He whom thou didst merit to bear, alleluia,\
Hath arisen as he said, alleluia.\
Pray for us to God, alleluia.

[2]{.v} V. Rejoice and be glad, O Virgin Mary, alleluia.\
[3]{.v} R. Because the Lord is truly risen, alleluia.

[4]{.v} O God, who gave joy to the world through the resurrection of thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ; grant, we beseech thee, that through his Mother, the Virgin Mary, we may obtain the joys of everlasting life. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.
