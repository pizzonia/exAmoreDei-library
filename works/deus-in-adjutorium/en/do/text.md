# Opening versicle {#main}

[1]{.v} V. O God, ✠ come to my assistance;\
[2]{.v} R. O Lord, make haste to help me.\
[3]{.v} Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, \* and to the Holy Ghost.\
[4]{.v} As it was in the beginning, is now, \* and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.
