# Ave Regina caelorum {#main}

[1]{.v} Hail, O Queen of heaven, enthroned!\
Hail, by Angels Mistress owned!\
Root of Jesse, Gate of morn,\
Whence the world's true Light was born:

[2]{.v} Glorious Virgin, joy to thee,\
Loveliest whom in heaven they see:\
Fairest thou, where all are fair,\
Plead with Christ our sins to spare.

[3]{.v} V. Allow me to praise thee, holy Virgin.\
[4]{.v} R. Give me strength against thine enemies.

[5]{.v} Grant, O merciful God, to our weak natures thy protection, that we who commemorate the holy Mother of God may, by the help of her intercession, arise from our iniquities. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.
